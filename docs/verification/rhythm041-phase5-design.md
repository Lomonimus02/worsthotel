# Automatic ordinary reservations — implementation contract

Prepared during phase 4 verification. This is design intent, not a completed feature or test result.

Reuse the eight existing deterministic enquiries per arrival date, dated one-night reservations, current/tomorrow horizon, physical check-in/key ownership and actual room intervals. A reservation is provisionally assigned a real room; staff can reassign it before arrival. Its identity, guest, dates and agreed price remain unchanged. A room closed to new sales can receive reassignment of an existing contract after ordinary availability checks.

`SalesSettings` is an optional final parameter of `OperationsSettings` and is disabled in raw historical fixtures. `SessionConfig` and the production asset enable it explicitly. Proposed initial settings: four rooms open, rate 180, demand 0.9, price elasticity 1.5, deterministic seed 73129. Day 1 enquiry decisions run 08:30–12:00 every half hotel hour; subsequent arrival dates receive decisions on the preceding day 16:00–19:30. Configurations must keep all decisions in their legal horizon and before arrival. These are starting values to measure, not guaranteed occupancy.

`RoomSalesPolicies` contains six rows: RoomId, OpenForSale, Price, Revision. `SetRoomSalesPolicy(actor, roomId, open, price, expectedPolicyRevision)` applies one validated row edit atomically. It never processes demand, changes existing prices or cancels stays. `ReassignBooking(actor, reservationId, roomId, expectedRevision)` uses the same future-reservation gate and self-excluding interval test as the forecast. Revisions start at one and cannot overflow; duplicate/no-op/stale actions do not mutate state.

At each decision time, consume the enquiry exactly once. Open interval-available rooms are considered by asking price then room ID. One stable seeded offer roll is compared with `clamp01(baseDemand * (referencePrice / askingPrice)^elasticity)`, with explicit finite handling of any allowed free rate. Changing a price or reopening a room never rerolls an already-decided enquiry. Dirty rooms remain future preparation obligations; real room readiness still gates check-in. Protected ledger capacity is never freed by deleting unfinished guests or items.

Two bounded `SalesDayCursor` records store arrival day and next offer index 0–8. Next decision time derives from immutable settings and the cursor. Ordinary ticks respect decision boundaries; reports/midnight retain processed decisions and introduce only the next horizon date. Mirrors carry and validate policies, settings, cursor and `HotelReservation.IsAutomatic` atomically. Automatic origin initially uses ActorId -1; later staff reassignment may identify actor 0/1 without erasing its origin. Manual origin never permits -1. Read-only replicas cannot create bookings.

Production UI offers room sales/rates, confirmed today/tomorrow reservations, legal assignment preview, cancellation and approximate pressure/revenue. It does not offer ordinary approval or repricing existing contracts. Preserve draft revision until apply or explicit refresh, so a partner edit cannot be silently overwritten. Wire commands append SetRoomSalesPolicy and ReassignBooking; new policy fields must be absent/default on unrelated commands. Host mode rejects obsolete manual acceptance and contract repricing in automatic mode.

Existing fixed-roster model/physical fixtures explicitly isolate sales while retaining production heat/needs behavior. New production activation, model demand, real controller and final executable SOLO/LAN gates run with automatic sales enabled. The old manually prescribed three-day matrix remains a workload counterfactual, not evidence that automatic demand sells those cohorts.

Ownership after phase 4 passes: Agency Life model/settings/booking/forecast; Agency Interface UI/core adapters/wire commands; Agency Situations meaningful tests, fixture isolation and controller scenario; Root snapshot validation, integration, verification drivers and phase gates.
