# Prototype 0.3.2 — Natural Service scope

Continue the existing one-floor hotel, art direction, Unity URP, Physics and Input System. Six guest rooms, three archetypes and three days. SOLO uses one staff rig; host-authoritative LAN supports a second computer. Local split-screen remains a development option. No replacement project.

Guests keep seeded schedules, traits, physical check-in keys, room activities, sleep, temporary outings and checkout. Temperature, factual noise and concrete room-condition causes remain the three situation families; patience and memory affect responses. Allocation, price and maintenance decisions still shape consequences.

The current correction adds natural communication over the existing service layer. A guest notices, may raise their own real radiator or tolerate discomfort, then may use the room telephone or visit reception. Staff can also start a room-door conversation. Exact concerns stay private until communicated. One source retains its causal identity; retries do not create new jobs. Answering, accepting help and actually resolving a cause are distinct.

Preserved work includes the boiler's load/wear/pressure and mechanical repair, two portable heaters, two electrical circuits, physical blankets/bulbs/luggage, visible radiator settings and lamp faults. Players handle dirty and clean linen themselves; there is no automatic housekeeper. Services include late checkout and wake-up promises. Cold recovery is measured after help; an item delivery does not automatically erase the cause. Compensation grants finite relief without changing physical conditions.

WAIT uses one real vote in SOLO or both staff in multiplayer and stops for meaningful contacts and infrastructure events. Hotel time advances while physics remains normal. Room preparation also works before service; dirty bookings are allowed, but check-in requires readiness and the proper physical key.

## Current milestone boundary

Natural Service refines the existing guest/service systems, with a small shared response record, simple gestures, real route anchors, bounded contact attempts and information controls. It does not create a full life simulator or a new animation framework. A stay with no service request is a valid result; frequency limits are ceilings, not task quotas.

## Excluded

Restaurant, pool, spa, bar, weddings, conferences, additional floors/elevators, employee hiring/wages/skills, crafting, inventory progression, food/hunger simulation, a large tool inventory, city simulation, procedural hotel gameplay, internet matchmaking/Relay, host migration, a story campaign and complex dialogue. Guest self-help does not repair the boiler, reset breakers or replace equipment. No mobile service app or text-message system is added.

## Acceptance boundary

The simulation must show consequences of prior decisions: a noise conflict avoided by a different room assignment, optional portable warmth with electrical demand, repeat tripping under unchanged overload and a natural multi-system risk on later days. Careful play may avoid catastrophe. Three days, settlement, maintenance and a fresh-session reset must remain usable. Automated state/physical paths support this assessment; two-person discussion, pleasant carrying, audio clarity, real-display performance and enjoyable pacing still require human playtests.
