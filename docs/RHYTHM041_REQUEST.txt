You are continuing development of the EXISTING Unity project:

THE WORST HOTEL EVER

Do NOT create a new project.
Do NOT rebuild working systems from scratch.

The project has already gone through several core-prototype iterations.

The next milestone is:

PROTOTYPE 0.4.1 — HOTEL RHYTHM & PRESSURE

This is intended to be the LAST major core-design pass before moving into
Growth & Progression.

The goal is NOT to add more content.

The goal is to fix the missing middle layer between:

"everything is fine"

and

"the boiler / electricity has failed."

After this milestone the existing hotel should already provide meaningful
operational gameplay even on a day where no catastrophic failure occurs.

==================================================
0. CURRENT PLAYTEST FINDINGS
==================================================

Recent full multi-day playtests show several important things.

WHAT IS WORKING:

- continuous calendar time is significantly better than 5-minute shifts;
- guest arrival / departure gives the hotel some life;
- boiler failure can produce good gameplay;
- electrical problems can produce useful pressure;
- temperature problems can connect guest needs with infrastructure;
- the player sometimes genuinely feels that the old hotel is struggling
  to support the amount of business being pushed through it;
- guest behavior is more believable than in earlier versions;
- Solo testing works;
- LAN co-op foundation exists;
- current visual direction is good and should be preserved.

WHAT IS NOT WORKING WELL ENOUGH:

1. There is still very little meaningful gameplay between
   "hotel operating normally"
   and
   "major infrastructure failure."

2. Boiler load can be high,
   but the player often cannot meaningfully intervene until it actually fails.

3. The player understands that 4 guests are safer than 6,
   but consequences before failure are not strong / clear enough.

4. Guests often do not meaningfully suffer from risky hotel operation
   until a major failure happens.

5. Electrical failure often appears around boiler failure,
   but the causal relationship is not currently clear to the player.

6. Quiet periods are often empty.

7. Night exists,
   but there is no proper staff sleeping mechanic.

8. Different parts of the day do not yet feel operationally distinct enough.

9. The booking system is currently annoying:
   manually approving ordinary booking applications is not enjoyable
   and does not feel like hotel management.

This milestone must address these specific problems.

==================================================
1. DESIGN NORTH STAR
==================================================

THE WORST HOTEL EVER should feel like:

"We operate an old hotel slightly beyond what it comfortably supports."

Not:

"We wait until a random machine breaks."

The central tension is:

MORE GUESTS
→ MORE REVENUE
→ MORE BUILDING LOAD
→ LESS SAFETY MARGIN
→ MORE OPERATIONAL PROBLEMS
→ MORE MANAGEMENT DECISIONS.

The player should often think:

"We can probably take one more guest."

followed by:

"We probably should not."

==================================================
2. THE MISSING CORE LAYER
==================================================

The hotel currently has too much gameplay at two extremes:

STATE A:
Everything fine.

STATE B:
Infrastructure failure.

We need meaningful gameplay in between.

The intended infrastructure progression is:

COMFORTABLE

↓

BUSY

↓

STRAINED

↓

OVERLOADED

↓

CRITICAL

↓

FAILURE

Failure is only the final stage.

The player should already be making decisions during:

BUSY
STRAINED
OVERLOADED.

PART A — PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE
==================================================

3. BOILER MUST BE MAINTAINABLE BEFORE FAILURE
==================================================

The player must NOT need to wait for the boiler to break
before meaningful boiler work becomes possible.

Introduce / improve PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE.

The boiler should expose:

Condition
Current Load
Rated Capacity
Current Stress
Maintenance State
Failure State

The player should be able to perform maintenance
while the boiler is still functioning.

==================================================
4. BOILER INSPECTION
==================================================

Provide a readable inspection interaction.

The player should be able to inspect the boiler and understand:

Condition:
Good / Worn / Poor / Critical

Load:
Comfortable / High / Strained / Overloaded

Stress:
Low / Rising / High / Critical

Maintenance recommendation:
None / Recommended / Urgent

Exact numerical values may exist in detailed UI/debug mode.

Do not make the player interpret obscure engineering numbers only.

==================================================
5. BASIC PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE
==================================================

Add a reasonable maintenance option before failure.

Example concept:

BASIC SERVICE

- temporarily shuts boiler down;
- takes meaningful in-game time;
- costs a modest amount;
- restores some condition;
- reduces accumulated stress;
- cannot be performed instantaneously while the boiler is fully operational.

Exact tuning should be configurable.

Example only:

Condition:
48% → 68%

Do NOT hardcode this exact value.

==================================================
6. FULL SERVICE
==================================================

Provide a more expensive / longer maintenance option.

Example:

FULL SERVICE

- boiler must be shut down;
- significantly restores condition;
- costs more money;
- requires more in-game time;
- is best performed during low-demand periods.

This should create a real operational decision:

"Do we stop heating now while demand is low,
or risk continuing until morning?"

==================================================
7. MAINTENANCE MUST HAVE REAL DOWNTIME
==================================================

Preventive maintenance should NOT just be:

click button
→ stat increases.

The boiler should actually be unavailable while maintenance occurs.

This makes time-of-day matter.

Night / midday low demand may become desirable maintenance windows.

==================================================
8. NO FAKE BUSYWORK
==================================================

Do not create a complex boiler minigame for preventive maintenance.

A short physical setup is enough.

The interesting decision is:

WHEN to take the system offline.

Not:

whether the player can complete a 60-second repetitive repair animation.

==================================================
PART B — LOAD CONSEQUENCES BEFORE FAILURE
==================================================

9. CAPACITY MUST HAVE GRADUAL CONSEQUENCES
==================================================

Currently the difference between safe operation and failure
is not visible enough.

Introduce clearer intermediate behavior.

Conceptually:

LOW LOAD

System meets demand comfortably.

------------------------------

HIGH LOAD

System still works.

Wear increases somewhat.

Safety reserve is smaller.

------------------------------

STRAINED

System begins struggling to satisfy all demand.

Examples:

- some rooms warm more slowly;
- distant / inefficient rooms begin losing temperature;
- boiler wear accelerates;
- stress accumulates.

------------------------------

OVERLOADED

Demand exceeds effective boiler capacity.

Examples:

- multiple rooms lose temperature;
- recovery becomes poor;
- guest radiator adjustments may worsen load;
- stress rises quickly.

------------------------------

CRITICAL

Sustained overload + poor condition.

Failure becomes likely / eventually inevitable
depending on current simulation model.

==================================================
10. DO NOT USE INSTANT THRESHOLD FAILURE
==================================================

Crossing 100% load must NOT instantly explode the boiler.

Use accumulated stress / overload exposure.

Example conceptual model:

Demand exceeds effective capacity.

↓

Stress rises.

↓

If demand drops:
stress begins recovering.

↓

If sustained:
stress becomes critical.

↓

Failure can occur.

The player must have time to recognize:

"We are pushing this too hard."

==================================================
11. CONDITION MODIFIES SAFE OPERATION
==================================================

A worn boiler should tolerate load worse than a healthy boiler.

But keep this understandable.

Example concept:

healthy boiler:
rated capacity remains close to nominal.

poor-condition boiler:
effective safe capacity is reduced;
stress grows faster;
wear increases.

Do not make the math unnecessarily realistic.

==================================================
12. GUESTS MUST EXPERIENCE STRAIN BEFORE FAILURE
==================================================

This is extremely important.

Taking too many guests should matter BEFORE the boiler explodes.

Example:

Six occupied rooms create high demand.

Room 104 has weaker heat retention.

Boiler becomes strained.

Room 104 temperature begins:

21
→ 20
→ 19
→ 18.

Cold-sensitive guest reacts.

They may:

increase radiator;
request service;
use blanket if available;
eventually complain.

The player now experiences consequences
without needing catastrophic boiler failure.

==================================================
13. OVERLOAD FEEDBACK LOOP
==================================================

Guest self-help can legitimately make infrastructure pressure worse.

Example:

Hotel becomes cold.

↓

Several guests increase radiator settings.

↓

Heating demand rises.

↓

Boiler becomes more strained.

↓

Weak rooms become colder.

This is GOOD emergent behavior.

Do NOT script this chain manually.

It should emerge from independent systems.

==================================================
PART C — REAL GUEST CONSEQUENCES
==================================================

14. RISK MUST COST MORE THAN NOTIFICATIONS
==================================================

Currently risky operation can produce calls,
but guest consequences are still too soft.

Guests must have meaningful outcomes from prolonged bad conditions.

Possible consequences:

- satisfaction loss;
- compensation request;
- partial refund;
- room relocation demand;
- negative review;
- EARLY CHECKOUT.

==================================================
15. EARLY CHECKOUT
==================================================

Add a simple early-checkout outcome.

This should be relatively uncommon.

Trigger only when:

guest is experiencing a serious problem;

AND

the problem remains unresolved for a significant time;

AND / OR

guest has already complained;

AND

guest personality / tolerance supports leaving.

Example:

Cold-sensitive guest.

Room severely cold for long duration.

Guest complained.

No meaningful response.

Eventually:

"I can't stay here like this."

Guest checks out early.

==================================================
16. EARLY CHECKOUT CONSEQUENCES
==================================================

Possible effects:

- partial or full refund;
- lost remaining room revenue;
- strong satisfaction penalty;
- negative review.

Do not make this catastrophic.

But it must make risky overbooking financially meaningful.

Example emotional result:

"We accepted the sixth guest for another $180,
then refunded most of it because the hotel couldn't support them."

This is desirable.

==================================================
17. DO NOT MAKE GUESTS AUTOMATICALLY LEAVE
==================================================

Early checkout is the end of an escalation chain.

Not:

room temperature dropped 1 degree
→ guest leaves.

Use:

patience;
severity;
duration;
guest traits;
previous service history.

==================================================
90. FINAL DESIGN PRINCIPLE
==================================================

The gameplay should NOT depend on:

"Something broke, now I finally have something to do."

The hotel should create decisions while it is still functioning.

Good example:

Boiler is at 82% load.

Condition is poor.

Six guests are arriving tonight.

The player can:

close two rooms from sale;

accept everyone;

perform maintenance now;

wait until night;

adjust heating;

take the risk.

Nothing is broken yet.

But the player is already playing the game.

That is the target for Prototype 0.4.1.
