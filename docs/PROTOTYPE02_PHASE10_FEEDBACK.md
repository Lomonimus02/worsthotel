# Prototype 0.2 — Phase 10 environmental feedback

This bounded pass preserves the original hotel kit and gameplay rules. It adds presentation to existing physical movement and authoritative simulation state; it adds no thermostat, phone chore, new repair sequence, acoustic simulation or fabricated failure.

## State and parameters

| Feedback | Authority and exact presentation parameters |
| --- | --- |
| Guest footsteps | Actual distance moved along `GuestPresentation` routes, one step per 0.9 m. Per-guest minimum spacing 0.22 real seconds; shared voice launch cap 0.055 real seconds. Existing original synthetic step, fixed pitch 0.93, gain `HotelFeedback.masterVolume × 0.18`, range 12 m. No sound while standing or waiting at a closed doorway. |
| Reception arrival | One bell when a physically arrived guest successfully changes `Arriving → WaitingForCheckIn`. Existing service bell is the authored origin. Original 1320/1980 Hz transient, 0.26 seconds, fixed pitch, gain `masterVolume × 0.36`, range 22 m; bursts limited to one per 0.45 real seconds. Restoring an existing waiting guest does not replay arrival. |
| Complaint phone | `Incidents.OnIncidentStarted`, which living situations emit at `Complaint`, not `Observed`. One original 740/1180 Hz modulated ring, 0.76 seconds, gain `masterVolume × 0.55`, range 26 m. A small original handset at reception wobbles ±2.3° and its lamp pulses for 1.6 real seconds. Shared complaint debounce remains 0.6 real seconds. The previous global notification ding is replaced, not layered. The phone requires no acknowledgment interaction. |
| Portable heater | Original two-second fan loop at fixed pitch 1, base volume 0.16, range 12 m. Starts only when the physical enabled tool has a valid room, is not carried, and `EffectiveHeatOutput > 0`. Switch-off, pickup, placement outside a room, power cut or missing model registration immediately stop it. Volume ramps in at 0.45 per real second. The normal switch produces a quiet local click. |
| Manual heater prompt | Two logical lines: current room temperature and “manual heat”; “Switch off when warm · Carry to move”. This exposes actual room temperature and makes the manual switch clear. No automatic temperature control was added. |
| Central radiators | Existing fin/manifold paint gets a per-renderer multiplier interpolating cold `(0.53, 0.72, 0.84)` to warm `(1.04, 0.98, 0.88)`. Blend = `clamp01(Boiler.HeatingOutput − room.HeatLoss / HeatTemperatureGain)`, quantized to 1% for updates. This represents delivered central heat; it does not claim to simulate radiator-water temperature. A portable electric heater does not warm the central radiator visual. No glow or particles were added to radiators. |
| Electrical room warning | Only a powered room on a circuit with `Warning && !Tripped` gets a gentle sinusoidal intensity/emission sag between 82% and 100%, period 2.4 real seconds. It never invents an outage; the actual trip disables the same room lights and devices. Other circuits and corridor lights remain unchanged. Original room light intensities are cached and restored after warning. |
| Debug electrical load | Physical panel reads `OVERRIDE`, effective breaker demand and `ACTUAL` consumer demand when an F2 override exists. Normal production demand remains labelled `LOAD`. |

`HotelFeedback.masterVolume` remains 0.65. Every new world cue uses the nearer of the two staff cameras: attenuation is `(1 − clamp01(distance / range))²`. This is a shared stereo mix with one Unity listener. WAIT affects event frequency and actor movement, never playback pitch; real-time launch limits prevent a burst of many accelerated footsteps. All clips are original deterministic synthesized placeholders, with no external audio assets. There is no background score or recorded music track; the loud-room activity uses an original synthesized eight-beat toy-radio phrase.

## Integration and lifecycle

- `HotelPolishBuilder.AddEnvironmentFeedback` calls `AddLivingFeedback`, wiring existing bell, six radiator bindings and the single physical heater, plus the small phone prop. There are no scene searches in the new runtime updates.
- `GuestPresentation` calls the step cue only after actual route movement and the arrival cue only after a successful real reception-arrival command. These calls do not change schedules, routes, room ownership, or WAIT.
- Phone, arrival and guest sound sources share the established pause/disable/reset cleanup. A new simulation clears phone animation and pending transient sound. Re-enabling presentation does not replay an existing complaint.
- Heater fan reuses one source and one clip across enable cycles and `NewGame`; state replacement stops the old loop before applying fresh state. Disabled tools and carried tools cannot deliver phantom fan noise.
- Electrical warning phase uses real Unity time and freezes on hotel pause. Disabling the panel removes only its temporary dim; it does not turn on a genuinely tripped room.
- Radiators restore original paint on component disable. Their dynamic renderers are excluded from static batching; geometry, colliders and original material assets are retained.

## Verification and limits

`Phase1PlayModeTests.EnvironmentFeedbackFollowsRealDeliveryWarningComplaintPauseAndFreshSession` is a focused diagnostic fixture. It checks player-two-only proximity, actual heater delivery and power loss, normal pitch, pause/disable reuse, bounded B-only warning dim, clearly marked circuit override, central radiator cooling despite local electric heat, `Observed` versus `Complaint` phone timing, and fresh-session cleanup without extra sources/clips. It uses explicit diagnostic placement, a labelled electrical override, a forced boiler fault and checked-in adapter setup to isolate feedback; it is not evidence of naturally balanced play. The Phase 10 scene-compilation gate passed, and the saved `Logs/p02-phase10-editmode.xml` and `Logs/p02-phase10-playmode.xml` were read directly: **175/175 EditMode and 28/28 PlayMode passed, zero failures or skips**, including this feedback fixture. These results precede the final display-only UI patch; its subsequent regression, Windows rebuild, three-day player tour and GPU inspection are separate delivery checks recorded in `VERIFICATION.md`.

Machine checks verify source state and lifecycle, not perceived loudness, sound localization or mix quality. No human listening result is claimed. Final human listening remains necessary, including separate staff positions and several simultaneous rooms. There is no wall occlusion, directional acoustic propagation, recorded speech, or independent audio output per player. The radiator tint and shared mix are deliberately inexpensive readability aids. Existing shower/radio loops, boiler steam/pressure sounds, staff footsteps, door sounds, breaker warning/trip/reset, and state-driven room device shutdown remain in place.
