==================================================
GUEST PRESENCE / ROOM LIFE PASS
==================================================

The current guest simulation is functionally present,
but visually and behaviorally too raw.

Guests currently feel like simulation placeholders rather than people
actually staying at a hotel.

Examples of current problems:

- room doors remain open;
- players can casually enter while guests are showering;
- guests may sleep while standing;
- activities do not have convincing physical staging;
- guests can behave strangely around furniture / rooms;
- room privacy does not exist;
- transitions between activities feel artificial.

This pass should NOT create a complex life simulator.

The goal is:

MAKE SIMPLE GUEST BEHAVIOR BELIEVABLE.

==================================================
1. ROOM OWNERSHIP
==================================================

When a guest checks into a room:

the room becomes their temporary private space.

Room should know:

AssignedGuest
OccupancyState
DoorState
PrivacyState

Example states:

Vacant
Occupied
GuestAway
GuestInside
CheckoutPending

The guest should behave as though they actually own the room temporarily.

==================================================
2. ROOM DOORS
==================================================

Room doors should physically open and close.

After entering:

guest normally closes the room door.

Do not leave every occupied room permanently open.

Door behavior should support:

Closed
Open
Locked

When guest is inside:

door should normally be CLOSED.

During private activities:

door should be LOCKED.

Examples:

Shower
Sleeping

Do not require full realistic lock simulation.

The goal is readable privacy.

==================================================
3. STAFF ACCESS / KNOCKING
==================================================

Players should not simply walk into occupied guest rooms.

Normal interaction with a closed occupied room:

KNOCK.

Guest can respond.

Possible contextual interactions:

Ask to keep noise down
Deliver / discuss something
Offer room change
Guest complaint conversation

If gameplay requires entering the room:

guest may allow access.

Later the game can have a staff / master key.

For now keep this simple.

There should still be a way to enter during emergencies,
but normal guest privacy must exist.

==================================================
4. ACTIVITY ANCHORS
==================================================

Do not let activities happen wherever the NavMesh agent happens to stop.

Each room should contain explicit interaction anchors:

BedAnchor
ShowerAnchor
RestAnchor
DeskAnchor
DoorInsideAnchor
DoorOutsideAnchor

Guest activities should move to the correct anchor.

Example:

Sleep

Guest walks to bed
→ reaches BedAnchor
→ transitions to sleep pose
→ remains correctly positioned.

Not:

Guest enters Sleep state while standing beside the bed.

==================================================
5. SLEEP
==================================================

Guests must not sleep standing.

For Prototype:

a perfect cinematic animation is NOT required.

Acceptable implementation:

Guest approaches bed.

↓

Simple transition / short animation.

↓

Character is positioned correctly on bed.

↓

Sleep pose / sleeping animation.

↓

Wake up transition.

If animation assets are limited,
a simple believable pose is much better than standing sleep.

Make sure:

body does not clip badly through bed.

==================================================
6. SHOWER
==================================================

Shower activity should feel private.

Flow:

Guest enters room.

↓

Walks into bathroom / shower area.

↓

Bathroom door or room privacy state closes.

↓

Shower begins.

↓

Player hears shower audio.

↓

Boiler / hot-water demand actually increases.

The character does NOT need to visibly stand naked in the shower.

For Prototype it is acceptable to:

- hide character behind shower / bathroom geometry;
- disable visible guest renderer while inside enclosed shower if necessary;
- position them at a ShowerAnchor;
- use audio + steam/water feedback.

The important thing is:

the hotel simulation communicates:

"Someone is currently using the shower."

Not:

"There is an NPC standing strangely next to a bathtub."

==================================================
7. PRIVACY STATES
==================================================

Activities should be classified.

PUBLIC:

Walking
Waiting at reception
Leaving hotel

SEMI-PRIVATE:

Resting
Watching TV
Working at desk

PRIVATE:

Sleeping
Showering

Private activity:

room normally cannot be casually entered.

If player knocks:

guest may refuse / respond differently.

Do not build dialogue trees.

Simple state-based reactions are enough.

==================================================
8. GUEST MOVEMENT QUALITY
==================================================

Fix obvious navigation problems.

Guests should not:

- stand inside doors;
- sleep in corridors;
- rotate constantly;
- walk into walls indefinitely;
- stack perfectly inside each other at reception;
- stop far away from interaction targets.

Use destination anchors and reasonable stopping distances.

Add simple recovery:

if guest cannot reach target after a timeout,
recalculate path / safely reset to a nearby valid point.

Do not allow one broken NavMesh path to permanently destroy a day.

==================================================
9. ACTIVITY TRANSITIONS
==================================================

Avoid abrupt:

Walking
→ instant Sleep
→ Walking.

Use lightweight transitions.

Example:

Walk to bed
→ face bed
→ sleep transition
→ Sleep.

Walk to shower
→ close bathroom
→ shower starts.

Leave room
→ approach door
→ open
→ exit
→ close door.

These can remain simple.

Believability is more important than animation quantity.

==================================================
10. ROOM AMBIENCE
==================================================

When a guest occupies a room,
the room should occasionally communicate their presence.

Examples:

TV audio through door.
Shower audio.
Footsteps.
Light visible beneath door.
Muffled activity.

Do not constantly generate noise.

These details make the hotel feel inhabited.

They can also support gameplay.

Example:

player hears loud TV before receiving a noise complaint.

==================================================
11. BEHAVIOR DEBUGGING
==================================================

Extend debug tools.

Selecting a guest should show:

CurrentState
CurrentActivity
Destination
AssignedRoom
PrivacyState
PathStatus
NextScheduledActivity

Developer controls:

Force Sleep
Force Shower
Force Rest
Force LeaveRoom
Force ReturnRoom

This is necessary for quickly finding broken guest behavior.

==================================================
12. DO NOT OVERBUILD
==================================================

Do NOT add:

complex relationships;
conversation AI;
full Sims needs;
bathroom bodily functions;
hundreds of animations;
procedural daily lives;
guest social networks.

The goal is only:

A small number of behaviors that look believable.

==================================================
SOLO PLAY / DEVELOPMENT MODE
==================================================

The game must also be playable with ONE HUMAN PLAYER.

This is important because frequent testing cannot depend on another person
always being available.

However:

do NOT redesign the whole game around solo play yet.

The main long-term fantasy remains co-op.

==================================================
13. ONE PLAYER SESSION
==================================================

Main menu should support:

SOLO

HOST

JOIN BY IP

Solo starts the same hotel simulation with one player.

Do not require a second local controller.

Do not spawn an inactive second player.

All normal hotel systems should continue functioning.

==================================================
14. SOLO IS NOT SPLIT-SCREEN
==================================================

Solo mode:

one player
one camera
one input device.

Multiplayer:

one player per machine.

Do not reintroduce split-screen architecture.

==================================================
15. CO-OP INTERACTIONS IN SOLO
==================================================

Some current mechanics require two simultaneous players,
especially boiler repair.

For SOLO TESTING,
provide a minimal alternate interaction path.

Do NOT create an AI teammate.

Do NOT redesign every cooperative mechanic.

Example:

Two-player boiler repair:

Player A holds pressure valve.

Player B resets machinery.

Solo version:

player can mechanically latch / temporarily secure the pressure valve,
giving a limited time window to run to the second control.

OR:

allow a debug-friendly solo sequence:
Valve → lock position temporarily → perform restart.

The solo solution should:

preserve the same underlying simulation;
remain somewhat stressful;
not affect multiplayer behavior.

Do not convert all co-op mechanics into single-button actions.

==================================================
16. SOLO BALANCE
==================================================

Do not deeply rebalance the entire economy yet.

For now:

same hotel simulation.

Potentially allow small configurable SoloAssist values for:

guest patience;
room preparation duration;
critical repair timing.

Defaults should remain close to multiplayer.

Put these in configuration.

Do not scatter:

if (solo) magic multipliers

through gameplay code.

==================================================
17. SOLO MODE PURPOSE
==================================================

Primary current purpose:

FAST DEVELOPMENT AND PLAYTESTING.

A developer should be able to:

launch game;
start Solo;
play an entire 3-day prototype;
experience guest simulation;
clean rooms;
handle reception;
fix problems;
test electricity;
test noise;
test boiler;
test checkout.

without needing another human.

Later it can be decided whether Solo becomes a fully supported public game mode.

Do not make that decision now.

==================================================
18. IMPLEMENTATION PRIORITY
==================================================

These tasks may be developed in parallel with the current correction pass.

Priority:

A.
Fix obviously broken guest staging:
sleeping, showering, room entry.

B.
Doors + privacy.

C.
Activity anchors.

D.
Guest navigation cleanup.

E.
Solo session boot.

F.
Minimal solo handling for explicitly co-op repair mechanics.

Do not delay:
keys,
room turnover,
noise counterplay

for a huge NPC polish project.

==================================================
19. ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
==================================================

This pass is successful when:

1. Occupied room doors normally close.

2. Guests do not sleep standing.

3. Sleep happens at the bed.

4. Shower happens in the shower / bathroom context.

5. Shower activity still affects hotel infrastructure.

6. Players cannot casually walk into every occupied room.

7. Knocking on an occupied room works.

8. Noise complaints can naturally lead to knocking on the source room.

9. Guest private activity is visually believable.

10. Guest movement does not routinely end in obviously broken poses.

11. Guest activities use explicit environment anchors.

12. The hotel sounds / feels inhabited.

13. A single player can start the game.

14. Solo does not require split-screen.

15. Solo can complete the existing three-day prototype.

16. Explicit two-person interactions have a minimal solo-compatible path.

17. No AI teammate has been introduced.

18. Multiplayer/LAN work remains intact.

19. Existing simulation remains the same underlying simulation in Solo and multiplayer.

20. This pass does not balloon into a life-simulator project.
