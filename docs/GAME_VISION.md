# THE WORST HOTEL EVER

Prototype 0.2 continues the existing hotel and asks whether two people enjoy running it close to its limits while their commercial decisions cause physical cooperative trouble. The hotel lives through staggered arrivals, room activities and independent infrastructure. A session has three days; wall-clock pacing with WAIT requires human measurement.

**Decide → commit → system reacts → prioritize → cooperate → accept consequences.**

Accept guests, assign six rooms and set prices. A shower loads the worn boiler; a noisy neighbor disturbs a business guest. Players can repair, relocate, ask for quiet, compensate or accept loss. A portable heater can improve local warmth while overloading a shared circuit. One housekeeper prepares rooms in an order the players choose. Revenue, wear and maintenance carry into the following day. No random chore generator.

The hotel is a warm, operating grand hotel, repeatedly repaired over decades: cream plaster, dark wood, burgundy carpet, aged brass and oversized mechanical controls. Thick walls, recessed doorways, layered mouldings and chunky furniture carry the forms. The supplied image informs proportions, readable silhouettes and friendly first-person presentation only; no copied characters, brands or airplane environment.

The desired moment: “We should not have accepted so many guests.” / “But look how much we earned.” Cautious play must remain possible; catastrophe is never mandatory.
